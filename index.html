EVA Case Simulation

Can EVA become sustainable without abandoning what made it different?

You are advising EVA’s founders at a March 2023 strategic crossroads. Over four decision periods, you will make choices about the company’s services, technology, governance, and growth model.

Simulation horizon36 months
Decision rounds4 sequential choices
Recommended time25–35 minutes

Case background

The simulation begins at the decision point described in the 2023 EVA case.

From ride-sharing startup to mobility platform

EVA launched in Quebec as an alternative to conventional ride-sharing platforms. Its cooperative structure gave driver-members a voice in pricing and policy, while blockchain technology supported transparency and decentralized data management.

A pandemic-driven pivot

COVID-19 weakened passenger demand and pushed EVA toward B2B last-mile delivery, beginning with enterprise partnerships such as SQDC. By 2023, its network had grown to approximately 5,000 drivers.

Growth created a new problem

Higher transaction volumes increased the cost and complexity of maintaining the blockchain infrastructure. At the same time, investors became less willing to provide additional funding through the existing cooperative structure.

Core issue statement
How should EVA redesign its business model to improve financial sustainability while protecting driver equity, transparency, and trust?

By the end, you should be able to:

How your recommendation will be evaluated

Your final score is reported on a 0–100 scale using four weighted outcomes. The model is intentionally calibrated so that the strongest available pathway scores 87/100, while weak combinations can fall to 0. You will see category results after each locked decision, but the underlying formulas remain hidden until the final debrief.

35%Financial health

Ending liquidity and the monthly operating result generated by your combined recommendations.

25%Mission alignment

Driver economics, cooperative influence, transparency, and continuity with EVA’s founding values.

25%Growth readiness

Capacity, technology scalability, access to capital, and the reach of your chosen growth model.

15%Execution feasibility

The number, pace, and complexity of changes EVA must implement while continuing to operate.

Before you begin

How to use the simulation

25–35 minutes

Your role and workflow

It is March 2023, and you are a junior strategy consultant advising EVA’s founders. Over a 36-month horizon, you will make four key recommendations to EVA’s leadership. For each decision, you must select the option that best addresses EVA’s core issue while weighing the relevant benefits, risks, and trade-offs. After each round, once you lock your recommendation and view its impact, you will not be able to return and change it.

1
Review the decision

Read the case evidence, opportunities, constraints, and risks presented for that nine-month period.

2
Select one recommendation

Choose the option you believe best addresses EVA’s position at that point in time and supports the recommendations you have already made.

3
Lock your recommendation

Proceed to the impact page when you are ready. Your choice becomes permanent and cannot be revised later.

4
Assess the impact

Review the simplified financial result, operating consequences, strategic scorecard, and the new situation carried into your next decision.

Simulation sequence

The 36-month horizon is divided into four nine-month periods. Each round asks you to answer a specific consulting question.

PeriodDecisionQuestion you must answerSuggested time
Months 1–9Service portfolioShould EVA preserve ride-sharing, become B2B-first, or exit B2C entirely?5–7 min
Months 10–18Technology architectureShould EVA retain blockchain, adopt a hybrid architecture, or migrate to cloud/SaaS?4–6 min
Months 19–27Governance structureShould EVA preserve cooperative control, introduce hybrid governance, or convert to a corporation?4–6 min
Months 28–36Growth modelShould EVA consolidate in Quebec, expand directly, license its technology, or use a partnership model?4–6 min
End of Month 36Final assessmentHow coherent, sustainable, and defensible is your complete four-part recommendation?5–8 min

What remains hidden

The detailed operating assumptions, financial values, and formulas are intentionally hidden while you make your recommendations. You will see their consequences after each round, and you can review the full assumption register only after your final assessment.

This structure asks you to reason from case evidence and trade-offs first, as you would in an early-stage consulting engagement with incomplete information.
